# Privacy Policy — Narrata

**Effective 28 July 2026**

Narrata is an audiobook player for Audiobookshelf servers that you host yourself.

**The developer of Narrata does not collect any data from this app.** There is no account to
create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind in the
app. Narrata has no backend service. It communicates only with the Audiobookshelf server whose
address you enter.

## Where your data goes

Everything Narrata sends leaves your device for one destination: **your own server**. That
includes your sign-in credentials, your listening progress, bookmarks, and playback sessions.
The developer operates no server, cannot see that traffic, and has no access to your library or
your listening history.

If the server you connect to is run by someone else, their privacy practices apply to the data
you send it. Narrata is not involved in that relationship.

## What is stored on your device

**Sign-in credentials** — your server address, access token, refresh token, and username are
stored in the iOS Keychain, protected so they are readable only after you first unlock the
device following a restart, and marked so they never sync to iCloud Keychain. Signing out
deletes them.

**Downloaded audiobooks** — stored in the app's private container and excluded from iCloud and
iTunes backups. Deleting a download, or deleting the app, removes them.

**Playback preferences** — playback speed, library view mode, and series grouping, stored
locally.

**A random installation identifier** — a randomly generated UUID created the first time you run
the app. It is not derived from your device, your hardware, or anything about you, and it is not
shared with the developer or any third party. It is sent only to your own server so that server
can distinguish playback sessions. Deleting the app deletes it; reinstalling generates a new,
unrelated one.

**Continue Listening widget data** — the title, author, cover image, and progress of your current
book are written to a shared container so the Home Screen widget and Live Activity can display
them. This stays on your device.

## Permissions

Narrata does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health
data, or calendar, and it contains no code that reads any of them.

## Children

Narrata is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
Since the app collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from a child user.

## Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or
delete personal data a company holds about you. The developer of Narrata holds no data about
you, so there is nothing to produce or erase. Data on your own Audiobookshelf server remains
entirely under your control, and you can delete it there at any time.

## Crash reports

If you have enabled sharing analytics with app developers in **Settings › Privacy & Security ›
Analytics & Improvements**, Apple may send the developer aggregated crash and performance
reports. These come from Apple, not from the app, contain no personal information, and are used
only to fix bugs. You can turn this off in iOS Settings at any time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an updated effective date.
Because the app collects nothing, no change can retroactively affect data already gathered —
there isn't any.
